# Clip prompts for the walkthrough

One continuous walk through the village at night, generated by hand in Runway's web app. Each clip starts on the exact last frame of the clip before it, so the clips have to be made **in order, one at a time**.

## Runway settings (every clip)
- **Video:** Image to Video, model **Gen-4 Turbo**, **10 seconds**, **16:9 (1280×720)**.
- **Start image:** the file named for that clip below. Leg 1 starts from the still; every later leg starts from a frame Claude extracts into `frames/`.
- **No end image.** An end image pulls the camera backwards and breaks the walk.
- Save each download into `raw/` as `leg1.mp4`, `leg2.mp4`, …

## The style (identical in every prompt)
> Stylized 3D animated-film render with hand-painted textures, a 17th-century village at night, mostly in deep shadow and drifting mist. Cool moonlight and faint magic glow in violet and lilac; warm amber firelight from windows, lanterns and torches. Eerie but inviting. No people, no text, no letters, no logos.

```
Stylized 3D animated-film render with hand-painted textures, a 17th-century village at night, mostly in deep shadow and drifting mist. Cool moonlight and faint magic glow in violet and lilac; warm amber firelight from windows, lanterns and torches. Eerie but inviting. No people, no text, no letters, no logos. Colour palette: near-black #0b0a0f, deep grey-violet #121118, night violet #24044a, ultraviolet #4403bb, lilac #be93f9, amber #e38411, ember #d62d14. The lower left of the image is dark and quiet. Centered composition, 16:9.
```

## Leg 1: the village → the gate
Start image: `raw/still-village.png`

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts. The camera glides slowly forward down the hillside path toward the village, the torch-lit wooden gate growing closer, houses and the moonlit chapel spire rising ahead with gentle parallax, mist drifting past. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide toward the open gate. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight, amber torchlight. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. No people, no text.
```

**Done:** `raw/leg1.mp4`. Its first frame is the landing poster (`frames/leg1_first.png`); it ends at the torch-lit gate.

## Leg 4: the lane → the bonfire on the village green
Start image: `frames/leg4_start.png` (leg 3's last frame before the cut, frame 160)

The fire burns on the green just past the clock tower, so the camera only has a few houses to pass. It's the "someone was just here" idea: the village has only just left the fire, mid-argument.

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts, no scene changes. Continue the same slow, steady forward glide down the misty cobbled lane toward the clock tower. Just past it, the lane opens onto a small village green where a crackling bonfire burns, ringed by empty wooden benches and surrounded by timber houses with amber windows. Abandoned in a hurry: half-full tankards on the benches, a cloak dropped on the cobbles, a stool knocked on its side, as if a heated argument broke up moments ago. The fire throws long, flickering shadows across the house fronts. The camera drifts slowly along the ring of benches and on past the fire. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide across the green toward a tall, stern timber courthouse on the far side. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight, warm amber and ember firelight. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. Empty scene, no people, no figures, no text.
```

**Done:** `raw/leg4_video_v3.mp4` (third try), used whole. No cuts: it glides down the lane, swings left past a house corner onto the green, past the bonfire and abandoned benches, and ends square on the courthouse doors (`frames/leg5_start.png`). v1 cut at frame 1 and v2 cut at 1.8 s; both are unused.

**Lesson:** name one destination. The prompt that named the lane, the green *and* the courthouse cut twice before a clean take.

## Leg 5: the courthouse doors → the empty trial hall
Start image: `frames/leg5_start.png` (leg 4's last frame, square on the courthouse doors)

One destination: inside the hall. The doors are already in front of the camera, so it only has to go through them.

```

Continue the same slow, steady forward glide toward the carved wooden courthouse doors as they swing slowly open, and glide through them into a tall, empty timber trial hall lit by rows of candles: wooden benches on both sides, a raised judge's bench at the far end, and a lone wooden stand for the accused in the middle of the floor, lit by a single shaft of violet moonlight from a high window. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide toward the accused's stand. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight, warm amber candlelight. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. Empty room, no people, no figures, no text.
```

**Done:** `raw/leg5_video.mp4`, used whole. No cuts: the doors open onto a candlelit trial hall with pews, banners and a shaft of violet moonlight, and it ends centred on the empty seat for the accused (`frames/leg6_start.png`).

## Leg 6: the trial hall → out through the moonlit window → the graveyard
Start image: `frames/leg6_start.png` (leg 5's last frame, the accused's seat)

**First try** (not saved): the camera pushed into the chair, then cut to the graveyard. The seat blocks the centre of the frame and no door is visible, so "past the seat to a door in the back wall" gave the model nowhere to go. **Lesson:** only send the camera somewhere it can already see in the start frame.

The only visible way out is the tall stained-glass window on the right, where the moonlight comes in. The camera turns away from the seat, toward it.

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts, no scene changes. The camera turns slowly to the right, away from the empty seat, and glides toward the tall stained-glass window on the right wall, where the shaft of violet moonlight pours in. As the camera nears it, the window's leaded panes swing open outward like doors, and the camera glides straight through the open window into the night outside, into a misty graveyard behind the courthouse: crooked, leaning headstones, bare twisted trees, and faint violet ghost-light hovering over the freshest graves. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide along the path between the graves. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight, the amber candlelight left behind. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. Empty scene, no people, no figures, no text.
```

**If it cuts again, split it in two:**
- **6a, inside only:** "...turns slowly to the right, away from the empty seat, and glides toward the tall stained-glass window... In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide toward the glowing window, which fills most of the frame." No graveyard in the prompt.
- **6b, from 6a's last frame:** "...the window's leaded panes swing open outward and the camera glides through into a misty graveyard..."

**Done:** `raw/leg6_video.mp4`, used whole. No cuts: it turns to the moonlit window, the panes swing open, and it glides out into the graveyard, ending on the path between the graves (`frames/leg7_start.png`). Its second frame is a touch darker than the handoff frame, a faint flicker at the step change rather than a jump.

## Leg 7: the graveyard path → the chapel (the last step, in two clips)

**First try** (not saved): it jumped straight to a chapel. The chapel isn't in the start frame, and "the mist thins to reveal" still asked the model to invent a building in 10 seconds, so it cut. The chapel should also be taller, after the reference: a tall, narrow Gothic Revival chapel in pale limestone (unwsp.edu "island chapel" photo).

**Fix:** a fog wipe across two clips. 7a ends in fog that fills the frame; 7b starts from that nearly empty frame and the fog clears onto the chapel. Each clip has one simple move, and the fog hides the change of place.

### Leg 7a: deeper into the mist
Start image: `frames/leg7_start.png` (leg 6's last frame, the misty path between the graves)

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts, no scene changes. Continue the same slow, steady forward glide along the path between the graves, into the mist. The mist grows thicker and softly brighter, glowing pale violet in the moonlight, slowly swallowing the headstones and trees until it fills almost the whole frame, with only faint dark shapes of the last headstones at the edges. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide into the glowing mist. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. Empty scene, no people, no figures, no text.
```

Save as `raw/leg7a_video.mp4`. Claude extracts its last frame as `frames/leg7b_start.png`.

### Leg 7b: the fog clears onto the chapel
Start image: `frames/leg7b_start.png` (7a's last frame, mostly fog)

```
Single continuous shot, no cuts, no scene changes. Continue the same slow, steady forward glide through the glowing violet mist. The mist slowly thins and parts like a curtain, revealing close ahead a tall, narrow Gothic chapel of pale weathered limestone at the top of a flagstone path: a steep pointed gable rising high into the night sky to a slender stone pinnacle topped with an iron cross, a deep pointed-arch doorway with a small glowing stained-glass mosaic above a heavy carved wooden door, and wide stone steps. Tall dark trees frame it on both sides. As the camera approaches up the path, the heavy door swings slowly open and warm golden candlelight spills out down the steps. In the final second, settle into a slow, steady forward glide toward the bright open doorway. Stylized 3D animated-film look, hand-painted textures, violet moonlight on pale stone, warm amber candlelight. Smooth, slow, graceful motion. Empty scene, no people, no figures, no text.
```

Both clips together make the chapel step's video (Claude joins them).

**Done:** `raw/leg7a_video.mp4` is the "Final Trial" video: it walks into the mist until fog fills the frame (its last frame is `frames/leg7b_start.png`). `raw/leg7b_video.mp4`, used whole, is the video behind "The village has accused you": the fog clears onto the tall Gothic chapel and its door opens onto a candlelit altar. No cuts in either.

**All the steps now have video.**

Every leg keeps the same opening and closing clauses: it **continues the same slow, steady forward glide**, and **in the final second settles back into a slow, steady forward glide** toward the next place.
