Witch Trial

A Guide for the Accused

Someone here is the Witch.

Host a Game Join a Village How to Play

The Village Gate

Entering the Village

One person hosts. They open the table screen on a laptop or TV everyone can see, or share it on a video call. It shows a village code and a QR code.

Everyone scans the code with their phone, or taps Join a Village and types it in. The host joins on their phone too. 5 to 12 players.

When the game begins, your phone tells you who you are. Keep it to yourself.

  • The Village wins by condemning the Witch at any trial.
  • The Witch and her Familiar win if she escapes every trial.
  • The Werewolf wins if the Witch Hunter is condemned and the Werewolf remains unknown.

Inside the Village

Cast Your Magic

Three times a game, every living player secretly casts one spell on their phone.

Scry
Learn a clue about another player.
Curse
Cloud whatever another player learns this round. The Witch’s Curse can turn it into a lie.
True Scry
The Witch Hunter, once per game: a clue no Curse can touch.
Vision
The Medium, once, in Round 2 or 3: a glimpse of who the Witch might be.

Any clue may be cursed. Trust no single omen.

The Bonfire

Gather at the Fire

After every round of spells, the village talks: 90 seconds after the first, two and a half minutes after the others.

Share what you learned, or don’t. No one can see anyone else’s phone, so every claim is only as good as the person making it.

“I scried Thomas.”

“That isn’t what you said before.”

“My result says she’s Supernatural.”

“Or you’ve been Cursed.”

The Courthouse

The Trial

Twice during the game, the village holds a trial. Everyone votes on their phone and has 60 seconds to do it.

The player with the most votes is condemned. A tie goes to one runoff; if it’s still tied, one of the tied players is chosen at random.

The condemned player’s role stays secret. They become a Ghost.

Condemn the Witch at any trial and the Village wins.

The Graveyard

The Dead Still Speak

Ghosts can’t speak aloud, vote or cast spells. But from the moment they’re condemned, they see every clue the living receive.

At each Séance, every Ghost sends the village one message of eight words or fewer.

The Medium asks one yes-or-no question out loud. Each Ghost answers Yes, No or Unclear, and only the Medium’s phone shows the answers.

The Chapel

Final Trial

After the last Séance, everyone has 45 seconds to name a suspect. The three most accused each get 20 seconds to plead their case.

Then everyone votes at once, and the condemned player’s role is revealed. If it’s still tied after a runoff, no one is condemned, and the Witch walks free.

Then every mask falls. Each phone shows its role, and who won.

One last thing.

The village has accused you.

Your final statement?

That is exactly what the Witch would say.

Prove it.
