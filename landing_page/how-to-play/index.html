How to play

Witch Trial: A Guide for the Accused

Welcome to the village.

One among you is a Witch.

Another serves them in secret.

Others carry strange gifts, hidden motives, or dangerous knowledge.

Before the final bell tolls, the village must decide whom to trust, and whom to condemn.

Entering the Village

One player opens the game on a shared screen and becomes the Host.

Everyone else joins on their phone using the QR code or village code.

When the game begins, each player is given a secret role.

Keep your role hidden unless revealing it serves your purpose.

Some players serve the Village.

Some serve the Witch.

One serves only themselves.

The Nightly Ritual

The game unfolds through several rounds of magic, suspicion, accusation and judgment.

I. Cast Your Magic

During a spell round, living players secretly choose an action on their phone.

The two most common spells are:

Scry
Peer beyond the veil and learn something about another player.
Curse
Interfere with another player’s magic while uncovering information of your own.

Certain roles possess rarer powers, including True Scry (Witch Hunter) and Vision (Medium).

But beware:

Magic does not always reveal the truth.

Curses can distort what players see, and the Witch’s magic can cause someone to receive information that is completely false.

A confident accusation may therefore come from:

  • an honest clue,
  • a deliberate lie,
  • a corrupted vision,
  • or a player who genuinely believes something untrue.

II. Gather at the Fire

After the magic is cast, the village gathers to discuss what has been learned.

This is where the real trial begins.

Share your clues.

Hide them.

Lie about them.

Challenge another player’s story.

Ask why someone targeted a particular person.

Watch who defends whom.

Remember what people claimed in earlier rounds.

The village survives through deduction… but deception is part of the game.

III. The Trial

When discussion ends, the living may be called to vote.

Choose the person you believe should face judgment.

If the village condemns someone, that player dies…

…but their story is not yet over.

The Dead Still Speak

Condemned players become Ghosts.

Ghosts can no longer:

  • cast spells,
  • vote,
  • or nominate suspects.

But the dead may still possess knowledge the living desperately need.

During a Séance, Ghosts can send brief messages back across the veil.

The Medium may also ask the dead a question, and each Ghost may answer:

  • Yes
  • No
  • Unclear

Listen carefully.

Death may reveal new truths… but a Ghost can still choose what to say.

The Final Trial

As the game nears its end, the surviving players enter the Final Trial.

Suspects are nominated.

The accused are given a chance to defend themselves.

Then the village casts its final judgment.

If the Witch is condemned, the Village triumphs.

If the Witch survives, the Witch and Familiar prevail.

The Werewolf follows a darker path of their own and may win independently if their secret objective is fulfilled.

Who among you is the Witch?
