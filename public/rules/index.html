How to Play

Hear ye, villagers. Read closely, for the Witch surely will.

1Goal

A Witch has crept into the village. The village must unmask and condemn her before the Final Trial is over.

But not everyone touched by magic serves the Witch. A Werewolf has their own agenda, a Medium speaks with the dead, and some players hold powerful gifts of sight.

Cast spells to gather clues, then choose what to reveal, bluff about what you know, and work out who can truly be trusted.

The Village: condemn the Witch at any trial.
The Witch's coven (the Witch and her Familiar): keep her hidden until the very end.
The Werewolf: see the Witch Hunter condemned while never being condemned yourself.

2Roles

Witch: escape every trial. Your Curse can twist a victim's clue into a lie.

Familiar: you know the Witch's true face. Shield her by any means.

Witch Hunter: sworn to the village. Once per game, cast a sight no Curse can cloud.

Medium: sworn to the village. You speak with the dead and may glimpse the Witch in a Vision.

Villager: sworn to the village. No magic gift, only your wits.

Werewolf: you howl for no one. You win if Witch Hunter is condemned and you roam free.

3Spells

Each round, cast one spell from your phone.

  • 🔮 Scry: peer into another player's soul for a clue.
  • 🪄 Curse: cloud whatever another player learns this round.
  • ✨ True Scry (Witch Hunter only): a clue no Curse can touch.
  • 👁️ Vision (Medium only): a glimpse of who the Witch might be.

Any clue may be cursed, so trust no single omen.

4Game timeline

  • The Drawing of Roles: secretly learn who you are.
  • Round 1: cast spells, then argue your case.
  • First Trial: the village condemns one soul. They become a Ghost, and their role stays secret.
  • Round 2: cast spells, then argue.
  • Séance: the Ghost whispers one message of up to 8 words.
  • Second Trial: another soul is condemned and becomes a second Ghost.
  • Round 3: the last spells are cast. Argue once more.
  • Second Séance: both Ghosts whisper from beyond.
  • Final Trial: the three most suspected plead their case, then all cast their vote.
  • The Reveal: every mask falls away, and the winners are known.

Ghosts may not speak aloud or vote, but they can see every clue the living receive.
